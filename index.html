Dot to Dot - Reveal: Privacy Policy

Dot to Dot - Reveal is built to keep your information private. In short: everything stays on your own phone. There are no accounts, and nothing is sent to us.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no sign-in and no account, and it does not send how far you have got, your settings or any other information to us or to any server. It has no adverts and no tracking. We have no way to see what you do in the app.

Where your information is kept

How far you have got with each picture, the mysteries you have solved and your settings are stored on your phone, inside the app. They stay there unless you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Backups

When you make a backup, the app writes a small file on your phone and then opens your phone's share menu so you can choose where to keep it, for example Google Drive, email or Files. The app does not upload your backup anywhere itself. Where it goes is entirely your choice, and from that point it is handled by whichever service you pick. When you restore, the app only reads the file you choose.

Purchases

If you buy Premium or a scene, the purchase is handled by Google Play. Payment is processed by Google, not by this app, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only records on your phone that the purchase is unlocked.

Permissions

The app does not ask to use your camera, photos, contacts, location or microphone.

Children

The app is a puzzle game for adults and children alike. It does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear here and, where required, on the app's store listing, with a new date below.

Contact

If you have any questions about your privacy or this policy, you can reach us at razzleedesignsco@gmail.com.
